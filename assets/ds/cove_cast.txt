# The Cove -- Nintendo DS cast.  Tile coordinates into cove.txt.
#
# Palms, coconut palms, boulders, rocks and bushes are NOT listed here.  They are
# derived from the tile they stand on -- T is a palm, Y a palm carrying coconuts,
# R a boulder, r a rock, b a bush -- so a tree is added by adding a character to
# the map and the two halves cannot drift.
#
# Rows are  Type  i  j  [variant]
#
#   [base]   placed on entry, and again after a death
#   [day2]   food and water -- never present at the same time as [day1]
#   [doors]  i j land_i land_j -- the doorway and the tile you use it from
