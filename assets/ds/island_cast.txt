# Destiny Islands -- Nintendo DS cast.  Tile coordinates into island.txt.

# Palms, coconut palms, boulders, rocks and lamp posts are NOT listed here.
# They are derived from the tile they stand on: T is a palm, Y a palm carrying
# coconuts, R a boulder, r a rock, l a street lamp.  On the SNES both halves
# were hand-written and agreed only because somebody kept them in step; a T with
# no palm on it is an invisible wall.  To add a tree, add a character to the map.
#
# Rows are  Type  i  j  [variant]
# The variant selects a line of dialogue, or one silhouette out of several for a
# prop.  Nothing reads it until M5; 0 is always valid.
#
#   [base]   placed on entry, and again after a death
#   [day1]   the raft errand's four pieces
#   [day2]   food and water -- never present at the same time as [day1]
#   [spots]  where the Heartless come up the night the island falls
