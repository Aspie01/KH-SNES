# The night the island falls -- Nintendo DS cast.  Tiles into ds/island.txt.
#
# The night runs on the two days' map, tileset and tilemap.  What makes it night
# is one palette upload -- that was true on the SNES and it is true here, which
# is why this scene emits no ground of its own.
#
# Props still come off the map, with one override: every Y tile gives a plain
# Palm here rather than a PalmC, because the coconuts were picked on day two.
# night.s spawned exactly that and called the two trees by the water "picked
# clean".  See NIGHT_PROPS in tools/build_assets.py.
#
# Two actors are deliberately absent because they are not PLACED, they are
# TRANSFORMED, and doing both would double them:
#   - the columns of darkness.  night.s reads Riku's position, deletes him and
#     spawns a Dark on the spot; then does the same to Kairi.
#   - the open door.  It retypes the Door already standing on the wall rather
#     than spawning a second one.
#
# Rows are  Type  i  j  [variant].
