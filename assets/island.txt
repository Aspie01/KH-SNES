# Destiny Islands -- the play space, 32x16 tiles of 16x16 pixels.
#
# Row index is j, column index is i, and the view is three-quarters overhead:
# +i runs right and +j runs down, so what you read here is what you see.
#
# Ground level:
#   ~  deep water     (blocked)
#   -  shallow water  (blocked)
#   .  sand           (walkable)
#   ,  grass          (walkable)
#   =  wood dock      (walkable)
#   W  waterfall pool (walkable) -- fill the bottle standing here
#   C  cave floor     (walkable) -- the Secret Place, behind the fall
#   T  grass, blocked -- a palm stands here
#   Y  grass, blocked -- a palm with coconuts to knock down
#   R  grass, blocked -- a boulder stands here
#   r  sand,  blocked -- a small rock stands here
#   b  grass, blocked -- a bush
#
# Raised, in eight-pixel steps.  A move is allowed only between tiles whose
# heights differ by at most one, so a deck two steps up can only be reached
# over its step tile:
#   B  wooden bridge  (walkable, +1) -- planks over the water it crosses
#   L  wooden step    (walkable, +1)
#   P  wooden platform deck (walkable, +2)
#   H  treehouse deck (walkable, +2)
#   M  leaning trunk  (walkable, +2)
#   K  treetop        (walkable, +3) -- the seagull nests up here
#   #  cliff rock     (blocked, +3)
#   F  cliff rock with the waterfall coming down it (blocked, +3)
#
# A raised tile is painted 8*h pixels above its cell with a cliff face filling
# the gap, which is what makes a ledge read as a ledge -- and it means the tile
# covers 8*h pixels of whatever is north of it.  Two rules follow, and the
# layout below obeys both:
#
#   * The cell(s) north of a raised structure must be blocked -- water, rock,
#     or more of the same structure.  One row for +1 and +2, two for +3.  The
#     Secret Place is therefore walled to the north and west and open to the
#     sea, and both wooden decks are built over the head of a cove.
#   * Nothing walkable above row 4, because a sprite standing on a +2 deck is
#     lifted 16 px and the HUD owns the top 24 px of the screen.
#
